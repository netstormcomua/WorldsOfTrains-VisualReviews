# Columns · two abandoned megastructure studies

Both studies run in the same playable salt basin on Layout A 2.2. The western Columns viewpoint, lens, ground-level underside viewpoint, train, rail spline, drive start and camera preset are shared. The mountain horizon remains visible beyond the structure. Selection is open.

| A · Transfer gantry | B · Broken ring collector |
|---|---|
| A 1.44 km longitudinal gantry on four pairs of concrete piers. Crossbeams, incomplete decking and three disused hoist frames form a long passage under the structure. | An approximately 1.2 km circular frame on nine inclined buttresses. The rail-level visit is the playable route in both studies. There are no stairs, lifts or connections to the upper deck. Materials and damage are at concept scale pending an architectural choice.

## Local checks

Mammoth travelled 1.708 km in each run. The train envelope remained clear in 155 checks for A and 156 for B. Vertical traces from the train anchor reached architecture in 109 and 35 samples respectively. The lowest sampled overhead height above the anchor was 137.85 m for A and 136.35 m for B. Foundations were checked against the saved terrain at the centre of each footing. Each public silent clip records 22 seconds of the moving train in DX11.

The integrated route display responded to the actual 1, 2 and 3 key events. With a second train at the junction, a request for Small was refused and Columns stayed selected. The HUD was hidden during filming. [Route indicator and refusal](route-hud.jpg) · [Runtime checks](checks.json)

The two demonstrations share the existing rail network. This review covers only the two local passages. The timing difference between earlier long and short routes remains open.
