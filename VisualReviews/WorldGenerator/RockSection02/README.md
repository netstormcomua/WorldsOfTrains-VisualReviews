# Outer 02 — wall and open salt ridge

[Wall](images/wall.jpg) · [Ridge](images/ridge.jpg) · [Same camera in baseline and revision](images/same-camera.jpg) · [Neighbor detail, before and after](images/neighbor-detail.jpg)

Mammoth approaches a continuous stepped wall with sand and talus along its foot. At the crest, the train-mounted camera turns toward a local salt basin below the track. The rail spline and track elevation match the [archived baseline](../RockSection01/README.md). The basin uses the approved Shore A material settings. The near shore has a visibly angular edge.

[Continuous DX11 drive, silent](outer-drive.mp4) · [Eight-second constructor sequence for the devlog](constructor.mp4) · [Rock groups hidden and shown from the same position](images/constructor.jpg)

The constructor sequence uses four real Unreal stills. The second shows the revised terrain with rock groups hidden. The wall and ridge are standalone versioned outcrop presets, composed in one test map. Two neighboring groups retain all 62 placement records and the original terrain samples across their footprints. The comparison detail shows one of those groups from the unchanged camera.

Mammoth covered 1020.4 m in 30.01 s and stayed on Outer. All 119 sampled rail checks passed. At sampled low and high mesh vertices, all 200 changed pieces crossed the traced ground surface. The smallest horizontal footprint clearance to the sampled rail centerline was 34.0 m.

The 95th-percentile frame time changed from 17.97 to 18.22 ms. Peak frame time changed from 25.15 to 46.05 ms. Both were single runs at 1440×810, DX11, 60 fps cap. The revision includes a 15 Hz salt reflection capture and a changing camera angle. These figures measure the complete scene.

Both early views used one gameplay load. From the saved first preset to the wall image took approximately 4 min 41 s, and to the ridge image 4 min 44 s. Full assignment-to-frame timing is unavailable. Artistic review of Outer 02 is pending.
