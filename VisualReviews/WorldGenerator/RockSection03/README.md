# Mountain ranges around the Outer salt basin

The selected 03b intermediate uses the original Hybrid D2 dry sand graph with photographed detail, normals and photographic side light. The early 03 version had little visible grain. [Dry sand close to Mammoth](images/dry-D2.jpg). The old D2 corridor mask is disabled outside its original coordinates.

The isolated map retains the Outer rail spline and Mammoth. Folded terrain forms the relief, with smaller sandstone ledges and talus from six native Rocky Desert meshes. All 666 instances have uniform XYZ scale. Profile curves, subsidiary spurs and a second mountain belt produce the skyline. Highest sampled terrain is 477 m above the local datum.

In 02, the old world-coordinate coast mask was saturated throughout the relocated basin. The apron also jumped to the pre-excavation ground at its edge. The 03 relief and optical transitions use one registered basin field. Damp sand gives way to uneven crust, with reflective water restricted to depressions below the common level. Of 1158 sampled grid edges crossing that level, none jumps by more than 5 m. Maximum vertical step is 0.94 m at 6.5 m spacing, versus 30.14 m at 7 m in 02. All neighboring tile seam samples match. The closest wet grid sample is 1996 m inside the outer boundary.

The crop uses (199,81) to (1437,691) from both 1440×810 ridge captures. Baseline and revision use the same two train camera placements. The lower shore view is a separate inspection camera.

Mammoth covered 1020.4 m in 30.01 s and passed 119 sampled route checks. Minimum conservative horizontal rock clearance to rail center was 46.4 m. For 596 of 666 checked rocks, sampled low and high mesh vertices straddled traced ground. A separate test of mesh vertex samples at their individual XY positions against the registered terrain confirmed intersection for all 666 pieces. Unavailable checks: 0.

DX11 at 1440×810 and a 60 fps cap. The 95th percentile changed from 18.22 to 18.24 ms, and maximum from 46.05 to 51.55 ms. One continuous run per version. The gameplay recording covers 24.97 s. Local regression checks cover the prior maps, six mesh dependencies, approved Shore A/B settings, tile seams, terrain fingerprint and wet mask.

Visual references were the native Rocky Desert Demo and Overview, the [Waterpocket Fold](https://www.nps.gov/care/index.htm) and [Badwater Basin](https://www.nps.gov/places/badwater-basin.htm). The upper slopes still look smooth and the local prototype is too small for the main map. This intermediate is retained for comparison. The next composition needs a larger asymmetric massif, a different secondary range and an open view across the salt.
