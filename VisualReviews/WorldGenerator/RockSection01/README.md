# Mammoth on the Outer rock section
Seven groups of ledges and talus flank the existing Outer rail spline in an isolated playable map. One seeded outcrop generator supplies the seven placements. The take uses 217 pieces from six Rocky Desert meshes.

[First view](images/first-view.jpg) · [Matched camera, rocks hidden above and visible below](images/control-and-rocks.jpg) · [16 seconds into the recording](images/drive-16s.jpg) · [Silent gameplay recording](outer-drive.mp4)

Mammoth covered 1,020 m in 30 seconds and remained on Outer. All 119 sampled route checks passed. Frame time was 17.97 ms at the 95th percentile and peaked at 25.15 ms in this single DX11 run. The recording covers about 25 seconds of the drive.

The first comparable view took 7 minutes 26 seconds from the start of the stage. Both stills came from one gameplay map load. The larger formation silhouettes repeat, and some ledges have a visible dark gap at their base. Artistic review is pending.

The group rows in parameters.json give longitudinal and lateral offsets from the spline reference, followed by width, depth and height, all in metres.
